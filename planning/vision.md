# Product Vision

## Purpose
Create a clear, professional online presence for Dr. Roberto Gonzalez Sanchez that showcases research, publications, projects, and contact information.

## Product Direction
- Static site (no server-side rendering required).
- Beautiful, impactful visual design with strong typography and clear hierarchy.

## Target Audience
- Academic peers and conference attendees
- Industry collaborators and potential partners
- Students and researchers exploring related topics

## Success Criteria
- Visitors can understand role and expertise in under 15 seconds.
- Publications and key projects are easy to find within 2 clicks.
- Contact and external profiles are clearly visible.
- Site loads fast and is usable on mobile and desktop.
