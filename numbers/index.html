FIELD NOTES

Everything is connected.

Place extractors on deposits, then draw belts toward the hub at 0, 0. Belts use a fine grid: each belt takes one cell, number sources take 3 × 3 cells, and the hub takes 4 × 4 cells. Built belts are continuous tracks. Press G to show the alignment grid. The arrow shows their output direction. One extractor activates a whole number patch; every outward-facing belt touching its edge gets one number every two seconds at level 1, independently.

Machine facing east. Ports rotate with the machine.

Arithmetic machines consume one number from each input. A enters at the rear; B enters on the left, relative to the output arrow. Invalid arithmetic pauses the machine. Inspect it, then clear its contents to recover.

Right-click a building (or select it after pressing Escape), then press R or click Rotate selected in its inspector. Rotation keeps the building’s contents. Right-drag from empty space to select multiple buildings, then choose Copy selected or Delete selected. Ctrl+C copies the selection; Delete removes it. The hub cannot be deleted. For a conveyor, choose Shorten route to find the shortest clear path between its run’s endpoints. Connections and numbers are preserved; Ctrl+Z restores the route. Junctions and closed loops are left intact. Extractors already output on every side.

Splitters accept a rear input and share it among three outputs. Mergers accept three inputs. Crossings carry two independent lanes. Drawing across a straight belt automatically inserts a crossing once unlocked; before that, drawing stops at the existing belt. Recyclers remove unwanted items.

Parallel contracts: start with one core contract. After 3 completed contracts, a second slot opens; after 6, a third opens. Core contracts unlock routing tools. Buy arithmetic operations in the workshop. Production orders ask for larger numbers and quantities. Deliver any active target to the same hub—its counter advances automatically. Completing one contract preserves every other counter and immediately opens a replacement.

Progression: first make 4 and 10 using only 2s and addition. Then spend contract XP in the Workshop on number sources, operations, or global extractor/operator speed upgrades. After unlocking at least 7 numbers and 4 operations, new contracts use reachable random targets from 1–10,000. In Inspect mode, drag an arithmetic operator to reuse it elsewhere; its contents and work move with it. With Conveyor selected, dragging from inside an operator draws a belt from its edge instead.

On touch screens, choose Pan to move the camera and pinch with two fingers to zoom, or use +/−. Choose Inspect and tap a building. Select area lets you drag a rectangle, then copy or delete its buildings. Touch controls also provide rotation, undo and paste. Contracts and Stats toggle the side panels.
