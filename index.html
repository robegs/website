About

I lead cybersecurity programs at NEC Laboratories Europe, guiding research that bridges rigorous measurement with practical defenses for the modern internet. My work spans cyber‑threat intelligence, online privacy, and data‑driven security strategies.

I hold an MSc (2011) and PhD (2014) in Telematic Engineering from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya.
