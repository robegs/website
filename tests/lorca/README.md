# Lorca educational page validation

The initial browser validation uses Playwright in Chromium, with iPad touch
emulation (810×1080 and 1080×810), phone (390×844), and desktop (1440×1100).
It is not a physical iPad/Safari test.

Checked end to end:

- All six timeline entries, including the age-appropriate account of his death.
- All five travel stops and their map highlights.
- Poetry comprehension, three expandable book cards, theatre activity.
- Five user-triggered audio notes, with successful Web Audio initialization.
- Word selection, generated text, and a UTF-8 poem download clearly marked as
  original workshop writing rather than Lorca's work.
- Four rhyming rounds and every quiz answer, incorrect-answer feedback,
  completion and replay.
- Every section at four viewport sizes, no horizontal overflow, visible buttons
  at least 44 CSS pixels tall, no missing assets or JavaScript errors.

Sources linked on the page:

- Biblioteca Nacional de España: author profile.
- Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes: biography, travels and artistic work.
- Fundación Federico García Lorca: later life and 1936.
- Centro Virtual Cervantes: American journeys.
- Chile's Ministerio de Educación / Currículum Nacional: the brief excerpt from
  «Mariposa». Only the opening two lines are reproduced.

Illustrations and creative game text were made for this site. The portrait is
explicitly labeled as an illustration. Downloaded poems are never uploaded.
